I'm Mina Habib, a Senior Data Scientist with more than 16 years in the industry.
I studied engineering at Cairo University in Egypt, and I've spent most of my
career since then building machine learning models of all kinds. Forecasting and
time series analysis are my specialty.

I started this site to write. Some of it will be about the work: how I think
about modeling, the trade-offs that don't make it into the final version, the
things I had to learn the hard way. Some of it will just be whatever I'm figuring
out at the time.

If any of that is useful to you, I'm glad. Thanks for stopping by.
